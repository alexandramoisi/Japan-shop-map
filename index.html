Good to know
  • Price key: ¥ about COS money or less, ¥¥ shirts around ¥20,000–30,000, ¥¥¥ splurge.
  • Since 1 November 2026 you pay the 10% consumption tax at the till and claim it back at customs when leaving Japan. Keep passport, receipts and goods together.
  • Issey Miyake costs roughly half the European price: a Homme Plissé Tailored Pleats 1 jacket is ¥63,800–66,000 in Japan versus €880–940 in the EU.
  • Back in Switzerland, goods over CHF 300 per person owe 8.1% import VAT at the red channel.
  • Pins are placed by hand and can be off by a block. Use the Google Maps link for directions.
  • Japanese sizes run small; many unisex labels use 1/2/3. Markets are cash only.
